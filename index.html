Our story

One oak fire.
One simple promise.

Ember & Oak began with a single wood-burning hearth and a belief that the best meals are unhurried.

We cook with local farms, season with patience, and let the flame do the talking. Every table is a small occasion, whether it is a quiet anniversary or a loud Friday. We want you to leave a little slower than you arrived.

  • Oak fireEvery dish starts at the hearth.
  • Local farmsGrowers we know by name.
  • No rushStay for one more course.
